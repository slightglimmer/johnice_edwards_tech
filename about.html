    Get in touch

    I'm pursuing a cybersecurity education and exploring opportunities in systems engineering, networking, cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence.

    What draws me to technology is the same thing that has always drawn me to houses and architecture: I want to know how things are put together. Why does this work? What's holding it up? What happens if I change this one piece? Networks, servers and cloud platforms are full of those questions, and I enjoy chasing the answers.

    How I got here

    My path hasn't been a straight line. I always wanted to be an architect, but that wasn't financially realistic for me at the time. Since then I've worked as a caregiver, earned my real estate license in Michigan, and bought my first home. I've also always been passionate about technology and automation.

    Building my homeownership website, Johnice Edwards Realty, gave me a taste of putting something real on the internet, from writing content to configuring DNS. Now I'm studying cybersecurity and building the technical foundation to go further.
